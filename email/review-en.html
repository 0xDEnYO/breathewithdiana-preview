Your breath reset.

Thank you for asking for this.

It is a short practice you can do lying down, wherever you are. It does not ask you to get anything right. It asks for a few quiet minutes, and for your willingness to feel what is already there.

Listen to your breath reset

It opens in your browser, so you can come back to it any time.

Before you begin
· Find ten minutes where nobody needs you.
· Lie down somewhere warm and comfortable.
· Headphones if you have them, so my voice is close.
· If it becomes too much, open your eyes and breathe normally. That is not failing, that is listening.

Afterwards you may feel lighter, or tired, or a little tender. All of it is welcome. There is nothing to do with it except let it settle.
